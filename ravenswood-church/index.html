Six Simple Steps

Here's How Your Morning Will Go

From the moment you pull up to the moment you head home, here's what to expect at Ravenswood.

01

Arrive and Park

We're at 4455 N. Seeley Ave., on Chicago's north side. Pull into the church parking lot through the alley off Sunnyside Avenue; it connects right to the church building. Coming by train? The closest CTA Brown Line stops are Damen and Montrose. Plan to arrive about 10 to 15 minutes early so you can get settled before worship begins at 10:30.

02

Stop by the Welcome Center

A friendly greeter will meet you in the lobby, answer any questions and help you find your way. If you don't have a Bible, just ask. We'd love to give you one.

03

Bring the Kids

Our nursery is open every Sunday for newborns to age 2. Kids' Church (ages 3 through 6th grade) meets during the morning service on the 1st and 3rd Sundays of each month, plus Bonus Sunday Kids' Church on 5th Sundays. Both are on the lower level, along with a private area in the nursery for feeding your baby.

04

Worship Together

We gather for worship, prayer and the preaching of God's Word. Sing along or simply listen. There's no pressure to do anything but be with us.

05

Hear God's Word

Our pastors preach through books of the Bible, one passage at a time. You're welcome to follow along in a Bible or just listen.

06

Say Hello

After the service, Pastor Duston would love to meet you. You can usually find him near the parking lot door stairwell. If you'd like to talk about following Christ or would like someone to pray with you, a pastor and his wife, along with a deacon couple, are available at the front.

Totally Optional

Let Us Know You're Coming

Totally optional. If you tell us when you plan to visit, we'll watch for you and help you find your way on Sunday.

Rather just show up? That's perfect too. You can fill out a Connect Card any time, in the seatback or online.

New Here FAQ

Questions People Ask

How long is the service?
Our Sunday morning service usually runs about 75 to 90 minutes.
Where do I park?
Pull into the church parking lot through the alley off Sunnyside Avenue. The lot connects right to the church building, so you can walk straight in.
What should I wear?
Wear whatever is most comfortable for you. Most people dress comfortably and simply.
What about my kids?
Kids' Church (ages 3 through 6th grade) meets during the morning worship service on the 1st and 3rd Sunday of each month on the lower level, plus Bonus Sunday Kids' Church on 5th Sundays. A nursery is available every Sunday for newborns to age 2, also on the lower level.
What if I need to feed my baby?
There is a private area available for you in the nursery on the lower level.
Where are the restrooms?
Restrooms are located on the lower level near the parking lot entrance.
What if I need a Bible?
If you do not have a Bible, we would love to gift you one! Just let us know when you plan your visit, and we'll have one ready for you.
Is Ravenswood part of a denomination?
Ravenswood Baptist Church is an independent church with no official affiliation with any organization or denomination.
Is there somewhere to find books and Bibles?
Visit the Resource Center in the lobby for Bibles, books and journals to help you grow in your faith. Each month we feature a recommended resource.
Can I watch online first?
Yes. Sunday worship streams live at 10:30 AM, and past messages are on our Sermons page.
What if I have more questions?
We'd love to help! Stop by the Welcome Center to speak with a greeter, or call us at 773.561.6576.

Since 1891

Our
Story

"Jesus Christ the same yesterday, and to day, and for ever." For generations, God has been faithful to His church on Chicago's north side.

How It Began

It Began With a Few Believers and a Shared Faith

From those early days, a church family began to grow, built not simply around a building, but around faith, fellowship, worship and service.

Generations have gathered here to worship, to pray, to serve and to grow together in faith. Each one has built on the faith and service of those who came before, and the story God is writing through this church is still being written.

Through the Years

A Legacy of Faithfulness

1891

A Church Is Planted in Ravenswood

In the spring of 1891, Rev. Charles Braithwaite, then pastor of Lake View Baptist Church, began meeting the Baptists who lived in the suburb of Ravenswood. Fifteen of them pledged to help start a church. Sunday afternoon services were held at the baseball park at Robey Street and Wilson Avenue, then in the Masonic Hall in East Ravenswood Park, with midweek prayer meetings in members' homes.

Oct 9

Ravenswood Baptist Church Is Organized

On October 9, 1891, with the help of pastors and members from sister churches, Ravenswood Baptist Church was officially organized.

1902

A Home at Sunnyside and Seeley

In 1902, the congregation built its church building at Sunnyside and Seeley Avenues, with the same arched window that still fills our sanctuary with light today.

1916

25 Years of Continuous Service

Under Rev. Fred B. Neel, the church marked its 25th anniversary with eight days of special services in October, celebrated in an "Anniversary Number" of the church paper.

1924

The Ravenswood Baptist Weekly

The church published its own newsletter, the Ravenswood Baptist Weekly, to keep the growing congregation connected.

1931

A Home-Coming Day

On Sunday, April 12, 1931, Ravenswood opened its 40th anniversary year with a Home-Coming Day: Bible School, morning and evening worship, an afternoon service and a supper together.

1933

Chicago's First Air-Cooled Church

Dr. F. H. Farrington became pastor in 1933. That summer, the Chicago Tribune reported that Ravenswood was said to be the first church in Chicago to install a cooling plant, and summer congregations grew as a result.

1941

50 Years of Ministry

The church celebrated its 50th anniversary with "4 Great Days" of services beginning October 9, 1941.

1958

A Long and Faithful Pastorate

Rev. Homer C. Resler began more than three decades of ministry at Ravenswood, serving from 1958 to 1986 and again from 1989 to 1991.

1972

Ravenswood Christian School Begins

Ravenswood Christian School began as a ministry of Ravenswood Baptist Church.

1976

A Building for the School

The school building was completed, giving Ravenswood Christian School its own home on Seeley Avenue.

1991

100 Years

On October 6, 1991, Ravenswood celebrated 100 years of ministry in Chicago.

2016

Pastor Duston Moore Comes to Ravenswood

Pastor Duston Moore and his family moved to Chicago to serve Ravenswood. His first Sunday was May 15, 2016, and his first verse-by-verse study at Ravenswood was the book of Philemon. In 2026, the church family celebrated his 10 years of ministry.

2026

135 Years of God's Faithfulness

On October 4, 2026, the church family gathered to celebrate 135 years of God's faithfulness, and sealed letters in a time capsule to be opened in 2041.

Today

The Story Continues

In worship and fellowship, in celebrations and ordinary Sundays, in serving our neighbors and teaching our children, God continues to work through His church. Different generations. One church family. One faith. One faithful God.

01The Bible

We believe the Bible to be the word of God. We believe the Bible is inspired and preserved by God, and that it is perfect and true. We use the Authorized Version of the Bible in all of our teaching.

Psalm 12:6-72 Timothy 3:15-171 Peter 1:23-252 Peter 1:19-21

02God

We believe in one holy and loving God, eternal, self-existent, infinite, and immutable, and that He has one nature, essence, and substance; yet He manifests Himself to man in the Trinity as the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit.

Deuteronomy 6:41 Timothy 1:17James 1:171 John 4:4

03Jesus Christ

We believe that Jesus Christ is God in the flesh and the only Saviour of mankind. We believe He came to earth, lived a sinless life, and offered Himself on the cross as a substitutionary sacrifice for the sins of all mankind. We believe He resurrected bodily and ascended into Heaven where He now dwells until His return.

Isaiah 7:14, 9:6, 43:11John 1:1, 3, 14, 18, 29Romans 3:19-25Romans 5:6-15Philippians 2:5-111 Thessalonians 4:13-181 Timothy 2:5, 3:16Titus 2:10-15Hebrews 7:26, 9:24-281 Peter 1:19, 2:21 John 1:3Revelation 20:1-6

04The Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit of God is a person of the Godhead who is presently indwelling and directing the development of believers and advancement of the gospel in the world. The Holy Spirit is sent to regenerate, sanctify, seal, and indwell all who have placed their faith in Jesus Christ.

Genesis 1:2John 3:5-6, 14:16Acts 1:5, 11:151 Corinthians 3:16, 6:19-201 Corinthians 12:13

05Sin and Salvation

We believe that all men are sinners by nature and are therefore separated from God. Faith in Jesus Christ is the only hope of salvation and redemption from this broken and fallen condition. Those who reject Jesus Christ as personal Saviour are separated from God for eternity, but those who receive Jesus as Saviour are adopted into God’s family and given His gift of eternal life.

Genesis 5:1-5Acts 4:19, 16:31Romans 3:10-23, 5:6-12, 6:23, 10:9-10Ephesians 2:8-9Titus 3:5-6Revelation 20:11-14

06The New Testament Church

We believe the local church is Jesus’ institution for advancing His gospel and doing His work in the world. The church is made up of those who have been saved and biblically baptized, uniting and faithfully gathering together for the purposes of Bible teaching, worship, prayer, fellowship, service, and observance of baptism and communion.

Matthew 16:16-18Acts 1:15, 2:41-43, 11:15, 20:281 Corinthians 15:51-58Ephesians 1:12-14, 5:25-301 Thessalonians 4:13-181 Timothy 3:4-15

07The Last Days

We believe in the literal interpretation of the Scriptures in context and with a dispensational view of biblical prophecy. This literal approach provides the following most likely timeline of future events: first, the rapture of the church, followed by a seven-year tribulation and the return of Jesus to the earth, followed by His literal rule of one thousand years ending with the Great White Throne Judgment, culminating with the creation of a new Heaven and new earth.

1 Corinthians 15:51-581 Thessalonians 4:13-18, 5:1-9Revelation 19-22

08Separation

We believe that a Christian should grow in grace, learning a lifestyle that is becoming of the gospel and honoring to Jesus Christ, properly reflecting His love and holiness. We believe in separating from biblical heresy and from sinful, self-destructive, and immoral behavior.

Romans 12:1-2, 14:132 Corinthians 6:14-7:12 Timothy 3:1-51 John 2:15-172 John 9-11Leviticus 19:281 Corinthians 6:19-20

09Creation

We believe that God supernaturally created the universe in six literal days, and therefore we reject any theories of evolution, theistic evolution, creation gaps, or intelligent design not attributed to the narrative of scripture and the power of God.

Genesis 1-2Exodus 20:11

10Civil Government

We believe that God has created structures of authority for the blessing of mankind, existing in three basic institutions: (1) the home, (2) the church, and (3) the state. God has given each institution specific biblical responsibilities and balanced these responsibilities with the understanding that no institution has the right to infringe upon the other, but that all should work in harmony under God’s word and His authority.

Romans 13:1-7Ephesians 5:22-24Hebrews 13:171 Peter 2:13-14

11Human Sexuality

We believe that God, for humanity’s flourishing, has designed the sexual relationship to exist exclusively within the bounds of lifetime marriage between a man and a woman. Therefore, any deviation from the biblical pattern of sexuality (homosexuality, lesbianism, bisexuality, bestiality, incest, fornication, adultery, and pornography) are harmful perversions of God’s gift of sex. We respect gender distinction as created by God, and we do not endorse the altering of one’s gender by surgery or appearance.

Genesis 2:24, 19:5, 13, 26:8-9Leviticus 18:1-30Romans 1:26-291 Corinthians 5:16:91 Thessalonians 4:1-8Hebrews 13:4

12Biblical Marriage

We believe that the only God-recognized marriage is the joining of one man and one woman.

Genesis 2:24Romans 7:21 Corinthians 7:10Ephesians 5:22-23

All people are welcome to attend our regular worship services. Those who attend may not display behavior that is indicative of the unbiblical sexual behaviors listed above in church services, at church functions, or on the church grounds.

Church representatives are not to display disrespect for those who are involved in sexual sin, though our teaching ministry will be consistent with our beliefs and the biblical patterns.

13Family Relationships

We believe that men and women are spiritually equal in position before God, but that God has ordained distinct and separate spiritual functions for men and women in the home and in the church. We believe that God has ordained the family as the foundational institution of human society, and that husbands and wives should grow in the biblical pattern of marriage and parenting.

Genesis 1:26-28Exodus 20:12Deuteronomy 6:4-9Psalm 127:3-5Proverbs 19:18, 22:15, 23:13-14Mark 10:6-121 Corinthians 7:1-16Ephesians 5:21-33, 6:1-4Colossians 3:18-21Hebrews 13:41 Peter 3:1-7

14Abortion

We believe that human life begins at conception and that the unborn child is a living human being, and therefore the life of the unborn should be valued as the life of any other human being.

Job 3:16Psalm 51:5, 139:14-16Isaiah 44:24, 49:1, 5Jeremiah 1:5, 20:15-18Luke 1:44

15Love

We believe that we should demonstrate love for others, believers and unbelievers alike. We respond graciously, gently, patiently, and humbly to those who oppose the gospel. The church should be an environment where unconditional love flows from God’s grace at work in every heart.

Leviticus 19:18Matthew 5:44-48Luke 6:31John 13:34-35Romans 12:9-10, 17-21, 13:8-10Philippians 2:2-42 Timothy 2:24-26Titus 3:21 John 3:17-18

16Missions

We believe that God has given the church a great commission to proclaim the gospel to all nations, and that salvation is made available to anyone who will believe on Jesus, regardless of nation, tribe, ethnic group, language, or culture. As co-laborers together with God, we desire to actively, generously, and faithfully engage in worldwide missions and church planting until Jesus returns.

Matthew 28:19-20Mark 16:15Luke 24:46-48John 20:21Acts 1:82 Corinthians 5:20

17Giving

We believe that every Christian is a steward of the material gifts of God entrusted to him. Every Christian should grow in grace, understanding the generous heart of God and learning to reflect that heart with generosity toward God’s church and toward those in need. This generosity should be driven by grace (not obligation), empowered by gratitude, and should flow from a willing and cheerful heart for the development of the church and advancement of the gospel.

Genesis 14:20Proverbs 3:9-10Acts 4:34-371 Corinthians 16:22 Corinthians 9:6-7Galatians 6:6Ephesians 4:281 Timothy 5:17-181 John 3:17

What Is the Gospel?

Good News
of God's Love
for You

The Gospel is the Good News! Walk through it one step at a time.

STEP 1 OF 8

God Loves You

God's love is personal. He sent His Son, Jesus Christ, into the world for you. Jesus lived among us, died on the cross for your sins, and rose again to give you eternal life.

For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.John 3:16
STEP 2 OF 8

We Are All Sinners

Every one of us has sinned and fallen short of God's perfect standard.

For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God.Romans 3:23
STEP 3 OF 8

Sin Has a Penalty

Sin leads to spiritual death, separation from God, but He offers you the gift of eternal life through Jesus Christ.

For the wages of sin is death; but the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.Romans 6:23
STEP 4 OF 8

Christ Paid for Our Sin

Jesus died in your place. God showed His love by sending Christ even while you were still a sinner.

But God commendeth his love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us.Romans 5:8
STEP 5 OF 8

Salvation Is a Gift

You cannot earn salvation. It is a gift from God, received by grace through faith.

For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God: Not of works, lest any man should boast.Ephesians 2:8-9
STEP 6 OF 8

Receive Christ Today

You can receive Jesus Christ right now by faith. Trust Him, believe His Word, and call upon Him for salvation.

But as many as received him, to them gave he power to become the sons of God, even to them that believe on his name.John 1:12
For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.Romans 10:13
STEP 7 OF 8

Pray From Your Heart

If you are ready, you can pray this prayer from your heart:

Dear Lord, I know that I am a sinner. I believe Jesus died for me and rose again. I now receive Him as my Savior. Thank You for saving me and making me Your child. Help me to live for You. Amen.
STEP 8 OF 8

God's Promises to You

If you have trusted Christ:

  • Your sins are forgiven (Colossians 1:14)
  • You are a child of God (John 1:12)
  • You are an heir of His blessings (Romans 8:16-17)
  • You have eternal life (1 John 5:12-13)

Put Off, Put On

Kurt Skelly opened Friday night with a reminder that marriage is the best place to practice your Christianity, and that the best gift you can give your spouse is your own walk with God. Then he walked couples through Ephesians 4:25-32, where Paul gives five behaviors that keep any relationship unified. Each one follows the same pattern: something to put off, something to put on, and a reason why. And the order matters. It starts on the inside, with honesty, and works its way out.

01Be Honest

Put off lying

Put on truth

Trust is the most important word in any relationship. If I can’t trust you, “I love you” means nothing. Keep your word, keep the door open with your money, your feelings and even your phone, and set aside time each day to talk about more than the weather. If trust has been broken, rebuild it by over-communicating, inviting accountability and giving it time.

Wherefore putting away lying, speak every man truth with his neighbour: for we are members one of another.Ephesians 4:25
02Fight Fair

Put off sinful anger

Put on righteous resolution

Marriage is the union of two sinners, so you will get on each other’s nerves. Know your triggers, watch for yelling or the silent treatment, and don’t let anger stack up. Pause and pray before reacting, use “I” statements, and deal with your own anger before bed, even if the problem isn’t solved yet. If anger feels out of control, get help from a pastor or counselor.

Be ye angry, and sin not: let not the sun go down upon your wrath: neither give place to the devil.Ephesians 4:26-27
03Be a Giver

Put off taking

Put on generosity

We can steal from our spouse without touching a wallet, by taking their time, their energy or leaving them to carry the spiritual load alone. Love gives. Put date night on the calendar, give one small thing every day without expecting anything back, make money decisions together and put the phone away when you walk in the door.

Let him that stole steal no more: but rather let him labour, working with his hands the thing which is good, that he may have to give to him that needeth.Ephesians 4:28
04Speak Life

Put off words as weapons

Put on words that build up

Watch for sarcasm, little digs and “I was just joking.” Listen to understand, then repeat back what you heard. Never complain about your spouse to others, and speak well of them when they aren't in the room. Affirm one specific thing about your spouse every day.

Let no corrupt communication proceed out of your mouth, but that which is good to the use of edifying, that it may minister grace unto the hearers.Ephesians 4:29
05Forgive Freely

Put off bitterness

Put on forgiveness

Bitterness keeps a record of wrongs and replays it. Forgiveness isn't keeping score. We forgive because God forgave us for Christ's sake, and when we're tempted to lash out, we choose to do something kind instead.

And be ye kind one to another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, even as God for Christ's sake hath forgiven you.Ephesians 4:32

What Is Baptism?

A Picture of the Gospel

Baptism is how a believer publicly declares their faith in Jesus Christ. It pictures what Jesus has already done: His death, His burial and His resurrection, and the new life we have in Him.

"Therefore we are buried with him by baptism into death: that like as Christ was raised up from the dead by the glory of the Father, even so we also should walk in newness of life."ROMANS 6:4
Why It Matters

Why Be Baptized?

Jesus Commanded It

Before He returned to heaven, Jesus told His followers to make disciples and baptize them. Baptism is a step of obedience for every believer.

MATTHEW 28:19

It Follows Salvation

Baptism does not save us. We are saved by grace through faith in Jesus alone. Baptism is the first public step after trusting Him.

EPHESIANS 2:8

It Joins Us Together

In the early church, those who received the gospel were baptized and added to the church. Baptism is a joyful celebration for the whole church family.

ACTS 2:41

If You Have the Gift Of

The Seven Gifts

Administration

You have been blessed with great organizational abilities. You derive great satisfaction from seeing projects completed. If you scored high in this area, every ministry at Ravenswood is looking for help in this area!

Exhortation

You have been blessed with the special ability to encourage others, by giving words of comfort, encouragement, and counsel in times of crisis. If you scored high in this area, you should consider being involved in an area of ministry where you can utilize your ability to encourage others. Being a part of our prayer ministry and our team of decision counselors are two examples of how God can use you to comfort, encourage, and counsel others.

Giving

You have been blessed with the ability to give material goods and resources with joy. If you scored high in this area, you should consider not only what ministries to support with your personal finances, but also how your example can motivate others to follow the Lord's leadership in their own giving. Do it with joy. Do not think just in terms of giving of your finances, but how you can give of your time to the Lord's work. We always need volunteers in the various ministries of Ravenswood Baptist Church.

Mercy

You have been blessed with the ability to have compassion for those who are suffering. If you scored high in this area, you should consider getting involved in our outreach ministry or sign up to cook a meal for a family in need.

Prophecy

You have been blessed with the ability to proclaim God's truth without compromise. If you scored high in this area, you should consider looking for a ministry where you can challenge others to live by God's Word. Understand that it will be more difficult for you than most to say what needs to be said with kindness and grace. Be careful that you do not offend with your approach. Looking into being a Connection Group leader or Sunday school teacher may be the place for you.

Ministries where God's Word is taught and proclaimed at Ravenswood are prayerfully overseen by our pastors. Share your results with a pastor, and ask God to open doors for you in His timing.

Service

You have been blessed with the ability to perform any task with joy that benefits others and meets practical needs. If you scored high in this area, you should consider the many opportunities here to serve: in our food services, in our building and grounds area and in our Connections Ministry (usher & greeter). You have the joy to serve, so ask God to open doors for you. And learn to say 'no' to the good things so you can say 'yes' to the best God has for you. Into the world: Ask God to show you where you can serve people, doing unprovoked acts of kindness, simply as an opportunity to demonstrate the love of Christ in a tangible way 'with no strings attached.' Use your home as a place to show hospitality.

Teaching

You have been blessed with the ability to explain God's truths so there is understanding and application in the lives of others. Inside the body: You should consider the opportunities in our teaching ministry, to possibly lead or help lead a Connection Group or Sunday school class. Into the world: You have a unique ability to communicate well. Ask God for opportunities to communicate with those who do not know Christ.

Teaching ministries at Ravenswood are prayerfully overseen by our pastors. If teaching is one of your top gifts, start by sharing your results with a pastor. They would love to help you take a next step that fits where you are in your walk with Christ, which often begins by serving alongside an experienced teacher. Ask God to open doors for you as you grow.

Giving FAQ

Questions About Giving

How does Ravenswood record my giving?
We use a church management program called Breeze for online giving. An end-of-year giving statement is emailed each February.
Is my financial information secure?
Yes. All payment processing is handled on PCI Level 1 compliant servers. Your information is held securely and is never given to third parties other than to process your contributions.
How do I schedule and manage my giving?
You can schedule, change or cancel contributions any time before the date they're scheduled. Once a contribution has been processed, it can't be canceled. Questions? Call the church office at 773.561.6576.
Credit card or e-check?
Both work. E-check transaction fees are much lower than credit card fees, so more of your gift goes to ministry.
Can I make a one-time gift?
Yes, online or by contacting the church office at 773.561.6576.
